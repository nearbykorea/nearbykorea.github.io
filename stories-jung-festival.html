THE FESTIVAL EDIT · JUNG

Korean food, shared in London.

Meet Kiwa’s winter street food, ONMA’s market cooking and the people behind JUNG Festival.

By Korea, Nearby · Published 11 November 2026 · Updated 19 November 2026

IN THIS STORY

Meet JUNGKiwa’s winter street foodMeet ONMAFestival dates & past editions

A market is a way into a culture: follow a flavour, learn a dish’s name, and discover the people making it.

JUNG Festival 정 페스티벌

JUNG is a Korean food festival in London, bringing food and independent traders together. Its first edition ran at King’s Cross on 1–4 May 2026. The name draws on jeong (정), the warmth and connection that can grow between people.

Rollin Lee and Market Root co-created the festival. If our Market Root introduction sparked your curiosity about Korean makers, JUNG offers another way in through food.

SEASONAL POP-UP · LONDON

Kiwa 기와 at Tod’s Festive Feast

A hot fish-cake skewer on a cold day is a small pleasure worth finding. Kiwa brings Korean bunsik (분식) street food to this winter market, presented by JUNG Festival.

Kiwa’s announced run: 19 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

By Tottenham Court Road station, London. The announcement shared with us places the stall in front of the station.

Announced hours: 11am–10pm, Monday to Saturday; 11am–9pm on Sunday. Check Kiwa’s latest update before travelling, especially over Christmas and New Year.

What to look out for

  • Tteokbokki 떡볶이 — spicy rice cakes.
  • Odeng 오뎅 / eomuk 어묵 — fish-cake skewers.
  • Giant gimmari 김말이 — fried seaweed rolls.
  • Buldak omelette — listed on the announced menu.

The announcement also mentions occasional specials. Ask the stall about that day’s menu, prices and allergens.

MEET A KOREAN STREET-FOOD TRADER

ONMA 엄마

If a roll of gimbap is your kind of market lunch, keep ONMA on your list. This UK Korean street-food trader introduces its food through gimbap 김밥, tteokbokki 떡볶이, jeon 전 and mandu 만두.

Think rice and seaweed rolls, spicy rice cakes, savoury pancakes and dumplings. The menu and location can change between pop-ups, so follow ONMA’s current announcements to find its next market day.

ONMA also appears in the official trader list for JUNG’s ARIRANG collaboration on 4–5 July 2026. That is a past event; it does not confirm participation at Tod’s Festive Feast.
