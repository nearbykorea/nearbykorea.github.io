Privacy & site statistics

Updated 11 November 2026. You can use Korea, Nearby without an account and without agreeing to optional statistics.

What happens when you allow statistics?

Your browser sends us the page name, the type of action (such as opening a map, choosing a category or following a link), the referring website’s domain and a broad device category. We use random browser and visit identifiers to estimate visitors and sessions. A visitor means a browser, not a verified person.

We do not include search terms, full referring URLs, URL query strings, names, email addresses, precise location or raw IP addresses in our analytics database. Browser identifiers are transformed on the server before storage. These are pseudonymous usage records, not an advertising profile.

Storage and your choices

Your statistics choice is saved in your browser for up to 180 days. If you allow statistics, a random browser ID lasts up to 30 days and a session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. We use browser local storage for these settings and identifiers. No analytics events or identifiers are created before you agree.

Choosing “No thanks” or withdrawing permission stops future analytics and removes the local analytics identifiers. Existing records age out of the 90-day reporting window. You can remove records linked to your current browser ID before withdrawing using the button below. After that ID has expired or been cleared, we cannot identify its earlier records from your browser.

Exclude visits from this browser

Site owners and anyone who prefers not to be counted can exclude this browser on this address. This setting does not transfer between website addresses. Your statistics choice still applies if you turn exclusion off.

Website addresses and older records

Statistics choices and browser IDs belong to each website address. If you visit Korea, Nearby at a new address, we ask for permission again and create a new random ID only after you agree. The same person may therefore count as two browsers across addresses. To remove records linked to the older address, use the original website’s privacy page in the browser that visited it.

Where the data goes

Statistics are sent to nearbykorea.sound88sound.chatgpt.site and stored in the existing Cloudflare D1 database through Sites hosting. Moving public pages does not reset existing records or extend their retention period. The page’s website origin is also stored to distinguish visits from different addresses. The dashboard is restricted to the site owner. Records older than 90 days are removed in batches when new analytics arrive or the owner opens the dashboard; only the most recent 90 days appear in reports. We do not send analytics to advertising networks.

The hosting provider necessarily processes requests to deliver the website and may keep operational logs under its own policies. Our analytics database’s exclusions do not describe every hosting or network log.

Other services

Some pages load Google Fonts. Opening a map requests tiles from OpenStreetMap. Links to Instagram, shops, schools and other external services are governed by those services’ policies. Signing in to the owner dashboard uses ChatGPT’s sign-in service and its necessary authentication cookies.
