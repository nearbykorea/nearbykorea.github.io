THE FESTIVAL EDIT · JUNG

Korean food, shared in London.

Meet JUNG Festival, its seasonal street-food pop-up and the next dates to look out for.

By Korea, Nearby · Published 11 November 2026

IN THIS STORY

Meet JUNGThe seasonal pop-upFestival dates & past editions

A market is a way into a culture: follow a flavour, learn a dish’s name, and discover the people making it.

JUNG Festival 정 페스티벌

JUNG is a Korean food festival in London, bringing food and independent traders together. Its first edition ran at King’s Cross on 1–4 May 2026. The name draws on jeong (정), the warmth and connection that can grow between people.

Rollin Lee and Market Root co-created the festival. If our Market Root introduction sparked your curiosity about Korean makers, JUNG offers another way in through food.

SEASONAL POP-UP · LONDON

JUNG at Tod’s Festive Feast

12 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Tottenham Court Road, London. JUNG’s official website announces a Korean street-food cart at the Christmas food market during this period.

This is a seasonal pop-up, separate from the next full JUNG Festival. Check the organiser’s current information for the precise location, opening days and hours, access and booking arrangements before travelling.

KEEP THE DATES SEPARATE

The next festival is still to be announced.

As checked on 11 November 2026, the organiser has not announced the next full festival’s dates. Its website offers a notification sign-up for future announcements.

King’s Cross (1–4 May 2026), the ARIRANG collaboration market (4–5 July 2026) and Korea Late at the British Museum (6 November 2026) are past events. Their venues and ticket details should not be used to plan a future visit.
