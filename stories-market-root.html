THE COMMUNITY EDIT · MARKET ROOT

Korean creativity, rooted in London.

Meet Market Root: a London market and community connecting Korean creativity, independent makers and curious neighbours.

By Korea, Nearby · Published 10 November 2026

IN THIS STORY

Meet Market RootThe community behind itFollow the next chapter

A piece of Korea can be something someone has made: a ceramic cup, a carefully sewn bag, a drawing or a new flavour. Market Root is one way to discover those stories in London.

Market Root 마켓루트

Market Root brings Korean and international independent creatives together through markets in London. Past editions have mixed art, fashion, design, food and lifestyle, giving visitors a way to discover smaller brands and meet the people behind them.

The official Copeland Park listing for Vol.7 describes a programme of independent brands, art installations and Korean food. That edition took place on 8–9 August 2026 in Peckham; it is a past event, rather than a current invitation to visit.

Background: Copeland Park’s Vol.7 event listing ↗ and August 2026 event coverage ↗.

MORE THAN A SHOPPING LIST

Get to know the makers.

In a 2025 interview, organisers Sujin Yang and Hyunju Kim described creating a place for Korean-born designers and creatives working in the UK to connect and share their work. Their account also explains how the market grew to include creators from other backgrounds.

That makes Market Root a natural addition to Korea, Nearby: a way into contemporary Korean creativity through the people making it part of life here. Our suggestion is to follow a maker whose work catches your eye, read their story and keep an eye out for the next opportunity to meet them.

Source: interview with Market Root’s organisers ↗ (October 2025).
