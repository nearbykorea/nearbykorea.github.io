THE SHOP EDIT · PLACES TO KNOW

Meet your next Korean food shop.

Oseyo, H Mart and Seoul Plaza: three names to know before your first Korean grocery trip.

By Korea, Nearby · Published 10 November 2026

IN THIS STORY

01 · Oseyo02 · H Mart03 · Seoul PlazaOpen the grocery map ↗

A Korean food shop is a good place to turn “I’d like to try that” into dinner. Take one recipe, write down its Korean ingredient names and give yourself a little time to browse.

These three names offer useful starting points around the UK. This is a source-based introduction to the shops, rather than a report from personal visits. Follow the official branch pages for current opening hours and product availability.

01 / THE CITY STOP

Oseyo 오세요

Oseyo brings Korean and wider Asian groceries together with snacks, household items and other ranges. Its official store finder covers locations in London and several other UK cities, making it worth checking before you plan a separate trip.

Make it a small first shop. Pick one recipe from Oseyo’s food guides, then look for the ingredients for that meal. The retailer says ranges vary by branch, so check with the individual store if your visit depends on finding a particular item.

02 / A NEW MALDEN STOP

H Mart 에이치마트

H Mart’s UK shop lists its New Malden address as Unit 1, Leigh Close, KT3 3NW. It also has an online grocery storefront, which is useful for looking through products before deciding how to shop.

Make an afternoon of it. Use the New Malden directory to find a Korean restaurant as well as the grocery stop. Check the venue addresses separately when planning your route. For an online order, confirm delivery coverage and any chilled or frozen delivery conditions at checkout.

03 / ANOTHER NAME TO LOOK FOR

Korea Foods & Seoul Plaza · 서울플라자

Seoul Plaza is the retail name used by Korea Foods for shops around the UK. Its range extends beyond Korean products to other Asian groceries, so it can be a useful place to look when a recipe needs ingredients you have not seen in your usual supermarket.

Start with the branch finder. Choose a specific shop on the retailer’s official page before travelling. Our directory currently carries a general Korea Foods and Seoul Plaza resource rather than verified pins for every branch.

Before you set off

Check the store’s current hours and your route. Map pins in our directory represent postcode areas, not exact entrances. Bring the ingredient name in Korean as well as English, and check product labels for ingredients and storage instructions.
